Consumer behavior keeps changing, often faster than a planning cycle can absorb. AI is reshaping how buyers discover, judge and choose. Platforms now ask for data infrastructure and measurement that most brands have never had to build, and the growth mandate for 2027 keeps rising while budgets hold flat.

Level’s experts expect that change to continue through 2027. Seeing it early takes data you can trust, and responding well takes creative and messaging that earns attention. Both matter in every one of the eight disciplines on this panel.

Think of this session as Level’s year-end broadcast: experts from across the agency on one screen, looking back at 2026 and ahead to 2027. It runs 45 minutes, with one prediction for 2027 from each discipline, then 15 minutes of live Q&A where you ask the questions.
